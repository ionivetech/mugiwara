# How is cost governed?

Token spend without a watcher grows until the invoice arrives. The Cost Governor makes spend visible, measurable, and auditable across every mission: budgets, context, work verdicts, scope discipline, and slop detection, recorded in one trail.

Example: a standard-lane mission crosses 1.5x its budget envelope mid-execution. The governor records a warn verdict with reason and evidence in the decisions trail, and the crew compresses output from the next flow stage. Nothing halted silently; the trail shows the signal and the response.

## What it watches

Lane budgets with warn and stop multipliers, context chars plus estimated tokens against the gate, work verdicts of skip, avoid, delegate, or complete per stage, scope drift with reuse and abstraction rationale, cognition discipline with bounded alternatives and deduped output, stop-slop detection across retry, healing, scope, context, investigation, and code signals, plus adaptive reservation, projection, and circuit breaking.

## Verdicts, not gates

Every governor is a pure verdict engine over explicit inputs; the crew acts on the signals under workflow rules, and each verdict lands in the decisions trail with timestamp, actor, reason, and evidence. Inspect through the cost command with JSON output or the report Cost section at archive. Expand scope with recorded acceptance and reason; expand budget with evidence plus a valid reason among scope, security, test, architecture, or healing. Debug by re-running the pure helper against the same inputs and comparing with benchmark thresholds.
